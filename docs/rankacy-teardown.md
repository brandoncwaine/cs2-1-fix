one match report, training tab. Mobile-width view, so desktop-only detail was not seen.

- Match report: per-player and per-round "impact" %, round event timelines, stats vs last-30 average,

  in the user's own LLM (not opened).

  Not tied to the player's own matches.
- Against our criteria: no single fix; round data exists but is not aggregated into patterns;
  no per-side advice seen; no fix tracking; price not checked.
