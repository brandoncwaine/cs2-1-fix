One-Fix

Invite only

One thing to fix per map and side, from your own CS2 matches

One-Fix reads your match demos after the game and looks for a habit that keeps costing you rounds. When it finds one, you get one card: the rounds to watch, a drill, and a number to beat.

Sign in through Steam

You need an invite code to join. You sign in on Steam's own site, so this site never sees your password.

How it works

  1. Sign in through Steam. Steam tells this site which account you are, and nothing else.
  2. Connect your matches. Enter your invite code and two codes from Steam's match history page: an authentication code and the share code of a recent match. The site checks them with Steam before saving.
  3. Play as usual. Once a day the site fetches the demos of your new matchmaking games and reads them. Nothing runs while you play.
  4. Open your cards. One card per map and side. Each claim on it links to the rounds it was counted from, so you can watch them yourself.

What to expect at first

A side of a map is analysed once you have played 30 rounds on it, which is about three matches. Until then its card shows how many rounds are left.

After that, most sides say “No fix found”. A fix appears only when the same thing happens in at least 4 rounds across 2 or more matches and the lost rounds are unlikely to be luck. When the data is thin the site says so and does not guess.

Before you sign in

Does this site see my Steam password?

No. You type it on Steam's own site and Steam only passes back which account you are. This site never asks for a password.

What can the two codes do?

They let the site read your list of matches so it can fetch the demos. They cannot sign in to your account, trade or change anything. Making a new authentication code on Steam cuts off access.

Who can see my cards?

You, and the site owner, who checks with each player whether their card is right. Other invited players cannot see them.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. “Delete my data” on your page removes your matches, cards and codes.

Does anything run during a match?

No. There is no overlay and nothing reads your screen or touches the game. The site only reads the demo file after the match is over.

Which matches count?

Matchmaking games that appear in your Steam match history, starting after the share code you enter. Steam removes old demos after a few weeks, so matches older than that cannot be read.
