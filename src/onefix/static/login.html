Questions

Does this site see my Steam password?

No. You sign in on Steam's own site.

What can the two codes do?

Only read your match list. A new code on Steam cuts off access.

Who can see my cards?

You and the site owner. Other players cannot.

Can I delete my data?

Yes, at any time, from your page.

Does anything run during a match?

No. No overlay; it only reads the demo afterwards.

Which matches count?

Matchmaking games in your Steam match history, from the last few weeks.
